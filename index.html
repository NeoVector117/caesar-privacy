Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026
Discord application ID: 1363198150688374954

This policy explains how Caesar ("Caesar," "the application," or "the bot") handles information when providing Discord community-management services. Caesar supports member audits, announcement and reaction tracking, event sign-ups, applicant management, and staff-facing records in Google Sheets.

1. Information processed

Depending on the features configured by a participating Discord community, Caesar may process:

  • Discord user IDs, usernames, and display names;
  • Server membership and relevant server roles;
  • Reactions, announcement responses, and participation records associated with configured activities;
  • Information users submit for event registrations, applications, or other configured administrative workflows; and
  • Message content where a configured workflow requires reading user-submitted Discord messages.

The information processed depends on the workflow and the permissions and API access available to Caesar.

2. Purpose of processing

Caesar uses information to audit membership, correlate registrations with Discord accounts and relevant roles, record announcement participation, organize event rosters, and let community staff review, filter, and manage applicants. It also converts relevant results into structured administrative records for Google Sheets.

Discord information is used for these community-management purposes. It is not collected for advertising, marketing, or unrelated user profiling.

3. Google Sheets and external storage

Caesar exports administrative output to Google Sheets. Records may contain Discord identifiers, usernames, relevant roles or membership information, reactions and announcement responses, registration details, and applicant-provided information.

Google processes and stores these records through its services. The Google account holders and community administrators responsible for a spreadsheet control its sharing permissions. Staff should limit access to people who need the records for the relevant administrative purpose.

Submitting information through a Caesar workflow can therefore result in that information being included in a staff-managed spreadsheet outside Discord.

4. Message content

Where a feature reads Discord messages, the purpose is to process responses relevant to the configured application, event, announcement, or administrative workflow. Caesar's stated purpose does not include indiscriminately archiving general Discord conversations.

Discord message content is not used to train artificial intelligence, machine-learning models, or large language models.

5. Sharing

Caesar does not sell Discord API data or provide it to advertisers or data brokers. Relevant records are made available to the staff administering the applicable community workflow and to services needed to provide that workflow, including Google Sheets. Any sharing of Discord API data must follow Discord's requirements, including obtaining a user's express direction where required. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

6. Retention

Records are retained for the duration needed to administer the relevant audit, event, application, or roster. Retention depends on that workflow rather than a single fixed period for every record. Information must be deleted when it is no longer necessary for Caesar's stated functionality, when the applicable user or Discord requests deletion, or when Caesar stops operating, unless applicable law requires retention.

Exported Google Sheets records require management by the people controlling those spreadsheets. Deletion is handled through a request and coordination with the relevant administrators; this policy does not describe an automatic deletion feature.

7. Access, correction, and deletion requests

Users can ask about their information or request correction or deletion by contacting Caesar's operator on Discord:

Discord username: Neocatus
If you cannot send a direct message, contact a staff member of the Discord server where you used Caesar and ask them to relay your privacy request to the operator.

Include your Discord user ID, the relevant server, and the event or workflow involved so the records can be located. Do not post private application answers or sensitive personal information in a public channel. Identity may need to be verified before records are disclosed or changed.

The operator will handle valid requests for records under Caesar's control and coordinate with the administrators controlling affected Google Sheets. Data will be deleted on a valid request unless retention is required by applicable law.

Choosing not to submit an optional registration or application avoids providing those additional answers. It does not automatically opt a user out of server membership audits or remove records already created. Use the contact route above for a privacy request.

8. Community administrator responsibilities

Community administrators configure Caesar workflows and determine who can access exported records. Administrators should tell users what information is requested, why it is needed, and who will review it, and manage the resulting records consistently with this policy, applicable law, and Discord's requirements.

9. Security

Caesar-related records must be protected against unauthorized access and disclosure. Spreadsheet access is governed by the relevant Google account and sharing settings. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Age requirements

Caesar is intended for people eligible to use Discord under Discord's applicable age requirements. If you believe Caesar holds information from someone below that minimum age, contact the operator to request review and deletion.

11. Policy changes

This policy may be updated as Caesar's features, data practices, or platform requirements change. The effective date will be updated when this policy is revised.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy or Caesar's data practices, contact Neocatus on Discord using the route described in section 7.
